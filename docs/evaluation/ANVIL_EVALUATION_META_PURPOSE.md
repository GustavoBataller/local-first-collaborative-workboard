# ANVIL_EVALUATION_META_PURPOSE

**Status:** ADOPTED — separate evaluation purpose  
**Authority:** Human/DO commission, 2026-09-22  
**Classification:** NOT Product Truth; NOT Product acceptance; NOT Product backlog

## Constitutive statement

This Product has independent Product and portfolio value. Separately, the reason for commissioning its development through Anvil now is to use the naturally arising engineering work as a real software-first evaluation environment for Anvil.

All material engineering work should therefore use the legitimate Anvil-governed execution path to the maximum extent available.

This meta-purpose must never alter Product scope, requirements, backlog, architecture, technology selection, complexity, failure modes or acceptance criteria merely to exercise Anvil or generate favourable study evidence.

Product decisions are made for the Workboard. Anvil evaluation observes how Anvil performs while delivering them.

## Operational consequences

- Ordinary Product work is selected because the Workboard needs it, not because it is useful to an Anvil study.
- Product backlog and Ready state are determined under Product authority independently of desired Anvil evidence.
- Product architecture and implementation stack are selected for Product fitness, not experimental variety or Anvil capability exercise.
- Anvil successes, failures, repairs, costs, blocked transitions and execution-environment limits may become evaluation evidence only to the extent they arise naturally while delivering legitimate Product work.
- A Workboard success does not by itself establish that Anvil is effective.
- An Anvil failure does not by itself establish a Workboard Product failure.
- If a legitimate Anvil path is unavailable for a transition, preserve that limitation honestly rather than distorting Product work or simulating Anvil execution.
- Evaluation claims must remain bound to the exact work, environment, evidence and property actually exercised.

## Prohibited contamination

This meta-purpose grants no authority to add or change:

- Product features;
- Product requirements;
- acceptance criteria;
- backlog items;
- architecture;
- language, framework, database or persistence technology;
- synchronization technique;
- fault classes;
- tests that exist only to improve Anvil study evidence;
- complexity or ceremony.

Any such change must independently earn its place under Workboard Product authority.

## Natural-work rule

The evaluation environment is the real Product development process.

The work universe is the ordinary Workboard backlog maintained for independent Product value.

An item becoming `Ready` must be explainable without reference to the desired Anvil study outcome.

No favourable Anvil result is required for Product completion.
